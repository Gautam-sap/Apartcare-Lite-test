# ApartCare Lite V6.5.21 — Login, Language, Password & Legal Hardening

## Included
- Expanded user-level language preference to 12 Indian-language options.
- Added script-appropriate font stacks for Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia and Urdu.
- Added presentation-layer translation coverage for major navigation, dashboard, reports, import and utilities labels, with English fallback.
- Improved apartment-selection cards when Account Number is blank: clear apartment name, Account Number and location spacing.
- Fixed first-login password-change persistence by synchronizing the legacy `admin_users` registry and tenant-scoped `tenant_users` registry.
- Password-change endpoint now persists state in a background task so large tenant state/apartment images cannot unnecessarily block the response.
- Increased frontend password-change timeout from 10s to 20s and improved timeout messaging.
- Added mandatory Terms of Service acceptance and Privacy Notice acknowledgement to new Apartment Account creation.
- Records terms/privacy versions and acceptance timestamp on the tenant account.
- Added Terms of Service and Privacy Notice pages as product templates requiring legal review before commercial launch.
- Added account-selection card styling and legal checkbox styling.
- Existing tenant isolation, calculations, navigation, RBAC, payments, expenses, dashboard and reporting APIs are unchanged.

## Legal note
The legal pages are product templates, not legal advice. Before commercial PROD launch, have an Indian-qualified lawyer review the Terms, Privacy Notice, data-processing arrangements, retention policy, grievance/contact mechanism and any applicable DPDP/IT obligations.
